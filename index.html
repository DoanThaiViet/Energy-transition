Priority 1

Existing oil & gas production and infrastructure

35POINTS
Improve existing fields and pipelines, and use their cash to fund new energy projects.
Why it matters for Vietnam
Vietnam still needs oil and gas. Domestic production helps the country rely less on imports.If investment stops too early, output may fall before other energy sources are ready.
Why PetroVietnam can compete
Knows its fieldsPeoplePipelinesFacilities
Use that experience to improve existing fields and focus on projects with reasonable costs and returns.
Course evidence
Lesson 3.2
Look at energy supply and cost together. Assets can lose value as demand changes.So we keep investing in oil and gas, but check each project rather than assume every field deserves more money.
Priority 2

Power generation, renewables and energy storage

20POINTS
Check buyers, grid access and expected income before investing.
Why it matters for Vietnam
Vietnam needs more reliable electricity. We look at gas power, renewables and storage together.A project only helps if it can deliver power when needed and connect to the grid.
Why PetroVietnam can compete
Gas facilities, offshore experience and people who manage large projects. We build on those for:
Gas power
Offshore wind
Projects for industrial customers
Course evidence
Lesson 2.2 · LCOE
A low production cost is not enough. We also ask:
What price can we sell at?How often does the plant run?Can the grid take the power?
Choices and tradeoffs 5 points

We delay large green hydrogen plants for export

Now · 5 points for trials and projects with real customer interest
Later · invest more when demand and returns are clear
Reason for the decision

A large plant needs affordable power, transport and buyers at the same time. We do not yet have enough certainty on these points to commit much more money.

We would rather learn with a small project first.

Main weakness
  • The plant may cost too much to run.
  • Buyers may not pay enough for the hydrogen.
  • Low operating hours or expensive transport could make it lose money.

Strategic fit

What Vietnam needs, what PetroVietnam already has

Vietnam’s needs
Energy security 3520

Keep domestic oil and gas supply and existing facilities working well. Add new power supply.

Course: why a country needs reliable supply, and why companies avoid projects that may lose value as demand changes.

Reliable and affordable energy

Save energy and combine gas power, renewables and storage. Choose projects with reasonable costs, reliable supply and grid access.

Lesson 2.2: consider the value of electricity and system reliability, not only production cost.

Emissions reduction 1015

10 points to save energy and cut methane. 15 points for selected CO₂ capture and storage. Renewable power also lowers emissions.

MACC: some measures cut both costs and emissions. CCUS cases: CO₂ is easier to capture from some sources.

PetroVietnam’s strengths
Knowledge of fields and reservoirs

Improve oil and gas production and find suitable places to store CO₂.

Better data and monitoring to show stored CO₂ stays underground.

Offshore experience and large-project skills

Build offshore wind and energy facilities. Sell offshore services to other companies.

More experience with renewable-project costs and new customers.

Gas facilities and industrial customers

Support gas power, energy trading and energy services for businesses.

Some facilities may need changes. New services must meet customer needs and earn a profit.

Assumptions and risks

Three assumptions, each with a decision trigger

01
Vietnam still needs domestic oil and gas through 2035
BasisExisting fields and gas facilities can supply energy and earn cash.
What could go wrongDemand falls faster than expected, imports become cheaper or emissions costs rise.
Decision triggerCheck demand, import prices, emissions costs and expected returns before approving new projects.
02
New power projects have customers and grid access
BasisPower receives 20 points. PetroVietnam can use its gas and offshore experience.
What could go wrongThe grid cannot accept the power, buyers are missing or the plant runs less than expected.
Decision triggerConfirm grid access, buyers and expected income before investing heavily.
03
CCUS and new services can earn enough revenue
Basis15 points each. Useful reservoir, offshore and customer experience.
What could go wrongCO₂ storage is not proven, nobody pays for CCUS or customers do not buy the new services.
Decision triggerProve safe storage and agree who pays. Test customer demand and profit before expanding services.
